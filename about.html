About Thorne Collective

Thorne Collective is a website made to showcase photographs and photo editing. The main idea of the website is to show how a photo can look different after it has been edited. It also gives a place where the original and edited versions of the photos can be viewed.

The photos can be changed using different editing techniques such as adjusting the colors, lighting, saturation, contrast, brightness, and other visual effects. These changes can make a photo look completely different from the original. Showing both versions makes it easier to see what was changed during the editing process.

This website was made as a way to collect and show different creative edits in one place. The gallery will contain the photos and their edited versions, while the other pages provide more information about the website and how to contact me. The website is kept simple so the photos and the editing can be the main focus.
